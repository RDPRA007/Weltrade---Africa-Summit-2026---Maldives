FTD and Level 1 Spread are weighted 50/50. Tournament Score = (FTD% × 50%) + (Spread% × 50%).

03 · Reaching 100%

Reaching 100% qualifies you for leaderboard consideration. It does not by itself secure a seat — final selection follows validation of results.

Worked example — how the score is built

Partner 99999 has an FTD target and a Spread target set from their own best result over the last 3 months.

Mid-tournament: FTD 65% · Spread 130%
Tournament Score = (65% × 50%) + (130% × 50%) = 97.5% — not yet qualified, 2.5 points short.
Close of tournament: FTD 90% · Spread 145%
Tournament Score = (90% × 50%) + (145% × 50%) = 117.5% — qualified, with an advantage of +17.5 over the 100% threshold.

The leaderboard ranks qualified partners by this advantage, highest first. Everyone below 100% is ranked by their score, showing how close they are to qualifying.

Partner Summit Selection Guidelines

These guidelines describe how partners are evaluated for consideration to attend the Weltrade Partner Summit. Your IB Manager will reach out to you with your FTD and Spread KPI.

These guidelines are internal rules explaining how the selection process works. They are not a contract and do not create any legal or financial obligation for Weltrade towards any partner. Meeting a target or appearing on the leaderboard does not by itself create a right to attend the Summit or to receive any compensation.

1. What this tournament is. Weltrade is running the Partner Summit Qualification Tournament, an internal evaluation and selection process open to the Top 54 Introducing Brokers ("IB" or "partner") in the Africa region. You compete against your own best result, not against other partners' business size. Your benchmark is based on your own performance over the last 3 months.

Reach 100% of your benchmark to enter the leaderboard. After that, every percentage point above 100% moves you higher in the ranking. The more you outperform your own benchmark, the better your chances of finishing in the Top 20 and securing a Summit invitation, subject to final confirmation under Section 4.

2. Your KPI. Each partner receives a KPI, covering two metrics: FTD (First Time Deposits) over the tournament window, and Spread over the tournament window (from direct client referrals). Your IB Manager will reach out to you and provide your KPIs.

3. How your score is calculated. FTD and Spread are weighted equally at 50% each. Tournament Score = (FTD Achievement × 50%) + (Spread Achievement × 50%). A score of 100% means you have reached your qualification threshold. There is no upper limit: the higher your score above 100%, the higher your position on the leaderboard. FTD and Spread can compensate for each other, for example 80% on one and 120% on the other still gives a Tournament Score of 100%.

4. Summit Invitation. Based on the final validated results, Weltrade intends to invite up to 20 top-performing partners to the Weltrade Partner Summit in the Maldives. The partner who finishes 1st may also be invited to bring one accompanying guest. Reaching 100% of your benchmark or ranking in the Top 20 does not automatically create an entitlement to attend the Summit. Final selection remains at Weltrade's discretion, following validation of results.

5. Leaderboard. The leaderboard is updated daily and shared in the dedicated WhatsApp channel for participants. It shows FTD Achievement %, Spread Achievement % and Tournament Score for each partner. The official data source is Weltrade's reporting system. In the event of any discrepancy, Weltrade's records prevail.

6. Key dates. Tournament starts 20 August 2026. Tournament closes 20 September 2026. Any activity outside this window does not count.

7. Conduct and grounds for exclusion. Weltrade reserves the right to exclude or disqualify any participant, or any specific clients, FTDs, deposits, or trading activity, where there are signs of suspicious, abusive, artificial, fraudulent, or manipulative behaviour, or activity inconsistent with normal client behaviour, when validating results.

8. General provisions. These guidelines describe Weltrade's internal process for evaluating and selecting partners for consideration for the Weltrade Partner Summit. They do not constitute a contractual offer and do not create any financial or legal entitlement to attend the Summit or to receive any related benefit. Weltrade may modify, postpone, suspend or cancel this qualification campaign, or the Summit itself, in whole or in part, at its discretion, for business, operational, compliance, regulatory or other reasons, notifying participants through the usual channels. Cancellation, postponement, non-selection, or any change to this campaign does not create any obligation for Weltrade to provide cash compensation, reimbursement, or any alternative benefit. Trading involves risk. This document does not constitute financial advice.
